NewPod Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026 · Effective: July 28, 2026

Summary: NewPod is a music player with no NewPod account system or advertising. The current version uses Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics and Firebase Analytics for app-usage measurement. It also supports Apple Music, files you deliberately select for import, and LRCLIB lookup when embedded lyrics are unavailable. Favorites, playback preferences, and imported local-audio information primarily stay on your device.

This Policy applies to official iOS, Android, and other releases of NewPod provided by Suben, Inc. (广州溯本信息技术有限公司) (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We are the controller/personal information processor for the processing described here.

Contact: kevin@subeninc.com. A third-party service is independently responsible where it determines its own purposes and means of processing.

3. Device Permissions

  • Media & Apple Music: reads and plays your system music library. Revoke it in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security.
  • Motion & Fitness: creates parallax effects. Revoke it in iOS Settings or disable the effect in NewPod.
  • Notifications: Android may request this permission for background playback controls. Disable it in system app settings.
  • File selection: NewPod accesses only audio files you deliberately choose through the system file picker.

5. International Processing

NewPod does not operate an overseas server that receives your personal information. Apple, LRCLIB, and Google Firebase may process the information described above internationally depending on service region, operations, and applicable law. If we later disclose personal information to another overseas recipient, we will provide legally required notice, obtain consent where required, and complete applicable transfer procedures.

6. Security and Retention

We use reasonable safeguards such as platform sandboxing, least-privilege access, and transport encryption. Apple Music uses Apple-provided interfaces, LRCLIB queries use HTTPS, and imported files are primarily processed on-device. No safeguard can guarantee absolute security, so please protect your device and original audio files.

On-device information is retained for the periods in Section 2. Clearing app data or uninstalling normally removes local app data, but operating-system backups, media-service data, and third-party records are controlled by the relevant provider.

7. Your Rights

You can access, correct, and delete local information and revoke permissions through NewPod and system settings. NewPod has no account to close; remove favorites, clear app data, or uninstall instead. You may also ask us to access, copy, correct, supplement, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to explain this Policy, subject to applicable law.

Email kevin@subeninc.com. We may verify only what is reasonably necessary to protect your request and will respond within the period required by law. Contact Apple directly for information it processes independently.

8. Children

NewPod is not directed primarily to children under 14. Minors should use it with a guardian's guidance. A child under 14 should obtain guardian consent before using online features involving personal information. If we learn that we processed a child's information without the required consent, we will promptly delete it or take other steps required by law.

9. Policy Changes

We will update this Policy when processing purposes, methods, data categories, or product features change, and provide an in-app or similarly prominent notice. Where law requires consent for a material change, we will ask again rather than treating continued use alone as consent.
